Heating Element Replacement

A burnt-out element is the most common reason a geyser stops heating. We test, confirm and replace it with an exact-fit OEM element.

  • Element testing
  • OEM replacement
  • Wiring & terminal check
  • Heating pull-up test

Thermostat & Thermal Cutout Repair

Water scalding hot, barely warm, or the cutout keeps tripping? Usually a faulty thermostat or thermal cutout — not a major fault.

  • Thermostat testing
  • Cutout replacement
  • Temperature calibration
  • Safety check

Leakage Detection & Repair

Dripping fittings, a leaking inlet/outlet pipe or seepage around the tank — we trace the exact source before quoting. Tank corrosion we assess honestly.

  • Leak tracing
  • Seal & gasket replacement
  • Pipe joint sealing
  • Honest tank assessment

Safety Valve & Pressure Relief

The pressure relief valve is the geyser's most important safety part. We test and replace it so the tank can never over-pressurise.

  • Valve testing
  • OEM valve replacement
  • Pressure check
  • Drain line check

Descaling & Sediment Cleaning

Scale and sediment coat the element and tank, making heating slow and bills higher. A proper descale restores performance and element life.

  • Tank flushing
  • Element descaling
  • Anode rod check
  • Efficiency test

Installation & Uninstallation

New geyser fitting or shifting homes — correct wall mounting, plumbing connections, earthing and a proper first heating test.
