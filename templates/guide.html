The 15-Day Filter Rule

Humidity and dust in Kochi can affect your appliance performance. Cleaning filters every 15 days prevents the compressor from overworking and improves air quality instantly.

The Perfect 24°C Balance

Setting your AC to 18°C doesn't cool faster; it just keeps the compressor running longer. 24°C is the human comfort sweet spot and saves maximum energy.

Clear the Outdoor Unit

Ensure the outdoor unit (ODU) has at least 2 feet of clear space around it. Blocking the fan prevents heat dissipation and can cause unit shutdown.

Category: Washing Machines

Fabrics & Machines Health

Avoid common mistakes that lead to drum damage and expensive motor repairs.

Longevity

Monthly Drum Descale

Hard water in areas like Whitefield and HSR causes white scale buildup. Use a descaler monthly to keep the heating element and drum surface clean.

Operation

Don't Overload the Drum

A full drum puts extreme pressure on the motor and belt. Always leave a hand's gap between the clothes and the top of the drum for proper washing.

Noise inspection →
Smart Use

Right Detergent, Right Amount

Using too much soap causes 'suds error' and damages the PCB. Use only Front-Load liquid for Front-Loaders and Top-Load for those specific machines.

Category: Refrigeration

Keeping It Cool & Fresh

Proper airflow is the secret to a long-lasting compressor.

Hardware

Vacuum the Coils

The black coils at the back (or bottom) of your fridge release heat. Vacuuming them twice a year prevents the compressor from burning out due to heat.

Compressor checkup →
Efficiency

The Paper Test for Gaskets

Close a paper in the fridge door. If it slides out easily, your rubber gasket is leaking air. This makes your fridge work 3x harder to stay cool.

Storage

Organization Matters

Don't block the air vents inside the fridge. Cold air needs to circulate. Overcrowding blocks the sensors and leads to uneven cooling.
