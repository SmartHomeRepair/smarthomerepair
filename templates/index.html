FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can a technician reach me?▾
Our average response time is under 2 hours for most areas in Kochi. In peak summer (March–May) it may be up to 3 hours. After booking, you receive the technician's name and live tracking via WhatsApp.
What warranty do you provide on repairs?▾
All repairs come with a 90-day service warranty. If the same issue recurs within this period, we fix it at no additional cost. Spare parts also carry the manufacturer's warranty on top of ours.
Do you use genuine original spare parts?▾
Yes, always. We source only OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) approved spare parts. We never use duplicates or low-quality components, ensuring your appliance performs reliably for years after every repair.
How is the service charge calculated?▾
There's a nominal visit/inspection charge of ₹399, which is waived if you proceed with the repair. Before any repair begins, our technician provides a complete cost breakdown. You only pay for what you approve.
Do you service all AC and appliance brands?▾
Yes — our technicians are trained on all major brands: Daikin, LG, Samsung, Voltas, Blue Star, Carrier, Hitachi, O General, Mitsubishi, Panasonic, Whirlpool, Godrej, IFB, Bosch and more. Both older and latest models.
What is an AMC and is it worth it?▾
An AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) is a yearly plan covering scheduled visits, priority support and repair discounts. Customers typically save 40–60% compared to pay-per-service pricing, and it significantly extends appliance lifespan.
Are services available on weekends and holidays?▾
Yes! Smart Home Repair operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including Sundays and public holidays. Premium AMC customers get emergency weekend support. We recommend early booking on Sundays as slots fill up quickly.
How often should I service my AC?▾
We recommend servicing your AC at least twice a year — before and after summer (March and October). For heavily used or dusty environments, quarterly servicing is advisable. Regular servicing improves efficiency and lowers electricity bills.
Do you handle commercial and office AC systems?▾
Absolutely — we specialize in VRV/VRF multi-split systems, cassette ACs and central cooling for offices, hotels, hospitals and malls. We offer customized AMC plans for businesses with dedicated account managers.
What payment methods do you accept?▾
We accept cash and UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm). Payment is made only after work is completed to your satisfaction. Digital invoice sent via WhatsApp/email.
